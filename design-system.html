Color

Grey is the screen, white holds content, purple is the text and selection, green is for actions. Click a swatch's Copy button to copy its hex value.

Contrast. White on primary-button (2.1:1) and text-secondary on white (3.3:1) are below the 4.5:1 guideline for small text. They are kept to match the Figma file: keep button labels bold and uppercase, and use text-secondary only for secondary information, never for anything a person must read to act.

Type

iOS system type: SF Pro Display for screen titles, SF Pro Text for the interface, and Roboto only inside category cards. SF Pro shows on Apple devices; elsewhere it falls back to the system font, then Roboto.

Space & shape

Screens are designed at 414 × 896. Layers are separated by borders and the grey/white contrast, never by shadows.
